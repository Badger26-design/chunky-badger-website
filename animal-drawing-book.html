What to Expect From a Chunky Badger Animal Drawing Book

Our animal drawing book series gives children everything they need to learn how to draw animals from scratch — no experience required.

Step 1–6 Visual Method

Every animal in the book is drawn using exactly six visual steps. There's no reading involved — just look at the picture and copy the shape. By step six, the drawing is complete and recognisable.

Practice Boxes on Every Page

Each page of our animal drawing book has a large blank practice area so children can attempt the drawing right alongside the guide. No extra sketchpad needed.

Colour & Make It Your Own

After sketching the outline, children are encouraged to bring their animal to life with colour. This is where real creativity flourishes — and where every book becomes truly unique.
